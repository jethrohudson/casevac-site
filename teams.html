Team pricing

For executive protection teams, details, and agencies buying CASEVAC Pro for the whole crew.

Who it’s for

Team pricing is for the person who outfits the detail — a team lead, security director, or agency buying for 5 to 18 or more people. Individual agents who pay for their own phone should use the in-app CASEVAC Pro subscription (monthly or yearly, with a 1-week free trial when Apple offers one).

What Team includes

Every member on the team gets all CASEVAC Pro features, including:

  • Shared live team map
  • Team map overlays, including motorcade routes
  • SOS team alerts and a lead-set rally point
  • Request Air Medical
  • The iPad side-by-side layout
  • The rest of Pro: Action Button routing, unlimited advance packs and principals, the LZ toolkit, flight weather and air time, and red night mode

Life-safety tools — nearest ER, routes, Call 911, and Share location — stay free on every phone, with or without Pro.

How pricing works

Team pricing is per team, with volume discounts. We quote it by email so it can match the size of your detail. We do not publish a team price on this page, and there is no lifetime unlock.

Single-user Pro is sold in the App Store as an auto-renewing subscription. Team plans are arranged with us, then each member uses the app under that arrangement.

Contact us

Tell us how many people you need to cover, your agency or team name, and whether you prefer a monthly or yearly term. We’ll reply with a quote.
